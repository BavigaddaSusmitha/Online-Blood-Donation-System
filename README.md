# Online-Blood-Donation-System
Online Blood Donation System

The Online Blood Donation System connects donors, recipients, and blood banks to provide blood quickly during emergencies. It helps manage donor records, blood requests, and blood inventory in an organized way.

This system allows users to register as donors, check available blood groups, and request blood when needed. Donors can donate multiple times, and their donation history is maintained. Blood banks play a key role by storing, managing, and supplying blood based on requests. The system also tracks blood inventory, including different blood groups and their quantities.

An important feature is the Health Check module, which ensures donor eligibility by verifying health conditions such as hemoglobin level, blood pressure, and lifestyle factors. This ensures safe and reliable blood donation.

The system uses a relational database where tables like donor, recipient, blood_bank, donation, blood_request, and blood_inventory are connected using primary and foreign keys. This maintains data consistency and accuracy.

Overall, the Online Blood Donation System reduces manual work, improves efficiency, and ensures fast access to blood. It enhances communication between donors and recipients and plays a vital role in saving lives during emergencies.
